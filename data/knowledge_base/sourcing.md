## Source quality metric
Judge a source by offer-acceptance and rejection rate, not application volume. A source with high volume but a rejection rate above 70% is a screening cost, not a quality channel. Synthetic demonstration guideline.

## Expected conversion by channel
Referrals typically convert best: expect 30-40% of referred candidates to reach offer stage. Recruiter-sourced candidates: 15-25%. Job boards: 5-10%. Campus: 5-15% and mostly for junior roles. Treat a channel far below its range as a candidate for deprioritising. Synthetic demonstration ranges, not industry benchmarks.

## Minimum sample size
Do not rank or deprioritise a source with fewer than 10 candidates or fewer than 3 resolved offers. Report it as low confidence and recommend collecting more data. Synthetic demonstration rule.

## Channel to role fit
Referrals and recruiter outreach suit senior and specialist roles such as ML Engineer. Job boards suit high-volume generalist roles. Campus suits entry-level Data Analyst and Data Scientist roles. Recommend shifting spend by role, not across the whole company. Synthetic demonstration guideline.
