## Offer acceptance target
Target an offer acceptance rate of at least 75% of resolved offers. Below 60% is a concern worth a root-cause review. Do not report a trend with fewer than 5 resolved offers. Synthetic demonstration target.

## Decline reasons
Typical decline reasons are compensation, accepted another offer, role scope, location, and slow process. If compensation is the top reason across at least 3 declines, review offered salary against the band for that role. Declines for accepting another offer often point to speed, not pay. Synthetic demonstration guideline.

## Offer speed
Target an offer within 5 business days of the final interview. Offers delayed beyond 10 business days are more likely to be declined for a competing offer. Synthetic demonstration target.

## Compensation bands
Compare an offer against the salary band for the same role and level only. Do not compare across roles. If a declined offer sits below the band midpoint and the reason is compensation, recommend a band review. Missing decline reasons must be labelled unknown. Synthetic demonstration guideline.
