## Stage SLA targets
Application to recruiter screen: 3 business days. Screen to technical interview: 7 days. Technical interview to written feedback: 2 days. Final interview to decision: 3 days. A stage is breached when the wait exceeds its target by more than 50%. Synthetic demonstration targets.

## Bottleneck rule
If a single active stage holds more than 40% of candidates and its average wait is above target, treat it as a bottleneck and review capacity at that stage first. A large stage count alone does not prove a bottleneck. Synthetic demonstration guideline.

## Role open too long
A role open more than 45 days without an offer should be flagged. Check the job description and sourcing mix before adding interview rounds. Synthetic demonstration threshold.

## Stage conversion ranges
Expect roughly 40-60% of applicants to pass the screen, 30-50% of those to pass the technical interview, and 50-70% of finalists to receive an offer. Compare stage conversion, not only totals. Do not claim time-in-stage unless entry dates exist. Synthetic demonstration ranges.
