## Where rejections should happen
Healthy pipelines reject most candidates at the screen and technical stages. If more than 40% of all rejections occur at the final stage, the earlier filters are too loose. Synthetic demonstration threshold.

## Job description problem signal
If one role shows many screen-stage rejections citing experience mismatch (above 30% of its rejections), the job description or sourcing is the likely problem, not the interview. Recommend rewriting the JD requirements. Synthetic demonstration guideline.

## Interview process problem signal
If rejections cluster at the technical stage for one role and cite the same skill gap repeatedly, check whether the interview tests what the job needs. If different interviewers reject at very different rates for the same role, review interviewer calibration. Synthetic demonstration guideline.

## Common rejection reasons
Typical recorded reasons are technical skills, experience mismatch, communication, and compensation expectations. Group by role and stage and separate observed counts from hypotheses. Never infer protected characteristics from feedback text. Synthetic demonstration guideline.
