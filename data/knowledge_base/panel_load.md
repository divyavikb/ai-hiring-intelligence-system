## Weekly interview limit
No interviewer should exceed their stated weekly capacity. Above 100% utilisation, recommend reassigning the excess interviews. Above 120% is urgent. Synthetic demonstration threshold.

## Underused interviewers
Interviewers below 50% utilisation are candidates for taking reassigned interviews, provided they have the right role expertise. Synthetic demonstration guideline.

## Rebalancing rules
Reassign only to interviewers qualified for the role and stage, and never move an interview in the final 24 hours without the candidate's agreement. Prefer spreading load over the whole panel rather than moving everything to one person. Synthetic demonstration guideline.

## Fatigue and quality
Sustained utilisation above 100% for two or more weeks tends to reduce feedback quality and delay written feedback, which feeds SLA breaches in the pipeline. Synthetic demonstration guideline.
